Financial Management – Case Study 1

BBA MJC-8 • Group 1 • 20 Marks

Case Study

Aarav Consumer Products Ltd. is considering two projects. Project A is expected to generate higher accounting profit in the first two years, but its cash flows are uncertain and the project has a higher risk. Project B is expected to generate more stable cash flows over a longer period, although its initial accounting profit is lower. The finance manager has to recommend the project after considering the objectives of financial management and the timing and risk of returns.
